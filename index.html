Kajurema Postagem

A private desktop tool that uploads and schedules videos on one YouTube channel: Kajurema.

What it does

Kajurema Postagem runs only on the personal computer of the owner of the Kajurema channel, a Brazilian channel about Stoic philosophy. After the owner reviews and approves a video produced for the channel, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • confirm that the signed-in account is the Kajurema channel (it refuses to upload to any other channel);
  • upload the approved video as private with a scheduled publish time, with its title, description, tags and category;
  • set the custom thumbnail and upload the caption file (.srt);
  • add the video to a playlist of the same channel, when the owner chooses one.

Nothing is published without the owner's manual approval.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not distributed, sold or offered to other people, there is no sign-up, and it does not access any other user's account or data. It signs in with Google OAuth 2.0, and the authorization is stored only on the owner's computer.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
